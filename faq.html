Frequently asked questions

Answers about dining on campus.

All four dining spots are located on the 1st floor of building C1 at Astana IT University.

Coffee Lake and Sheker are open on Saturdays. Coffee Lake operates from 9:00 to 20:00, and Sheker from 10:00 to 19:30.

No. All four dining spots are closed on Sundays.

You can visit the Menu page or use the View menu links on the dining spot cards.

Cafeteria in C1.3 serves hot dishes, soups, salads, bakery and dairy products. Open its menu to compare the listed portions and prices.

Coffee Lake in the C1.2 atrium lists iced coffee and iced tea on its menu, alongside sandwiches and ready-to-go meals.

Sheker offers cakes, croissants and sandwiches between C1.1 and C1.2. Cafeteria also has a bakery selection.

Use Coffee Planner on the Menu page. Choose hot, iced or any coffee, then enter your budget in tenge to see matching Americano and Latte options from the selected menu items.

Some menu groups are marked Demo additions and contain illustrative items. Check the notes on the Menu page and confirm prices and availability at the counter.

The Map page shows Cafeteria, Coffee Lake, Sheker and Mokko across C1.1, C1.2 and C1.3 on the first floor.
