Dining Spots

Find a full lunch, your usual coffee, or something sweet between classes.

All four spots are on the 1st floor of building C1.

The spots

Cafeteria

Hot dishes, soups, salads, fresh bakery and dairy at the lowest prices on campus. The biggest and busiest spot, so expect a line at lunchtime.

  • Salads
  • Soups
  • Hot dishes
  • Bakery
  • Dairy & drinks

C1.3

Mon–Fri · 9:00–20:00

Coffee Lake

Iced coffee and tea, sandwiches and ready-to-go meals in a cozy indoor corner. Good for sitting with a laptop.

  • Iced coffee
  • Iced tea
  • Sandwiches
  • Ready-to-go

C1.2 (atrium)

Mon–Sat · 9:00–20:00

Sheker

Hot coffee, cakes, croissants, sandwiches and salads from an open kiosk with small tables.

  • Sweets
  • Sandwiches & salads

Between C1.1 and C1.2

Mon–Sat · 10:00–19:30

Mokko

Classic coffee, packaged sandwiches and salads, chocolate and snacks from a small open kiosk.

  • Sandwiches & salads
